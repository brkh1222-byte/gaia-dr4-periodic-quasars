## New since 24 Nov
- **Wu, Q. 2026, "A pre-registered forward test of 86 ZTF periodic-quasar candidates finds no
  predictive periodicity", Zenodo, 27 Nov 2026, doi:10.5281/zenodo.24580702** (record verified via
  the Zenodo API on 28 Nov; not on arXiv; unrefereed; we read the abstract only).
  Freezes the Chen+2024 sinusoids on ZTF DR11 and scores them on the next 3.6 yr of ZTF r-band
  data against DRW mocks; 7/86 pass at p < 0.05 (4.3 expected).
  **Overlap: partial.** Same idea (frozen ephemeris, forward test, DRW null, pre-registered) on a
  different sample (ZTF-era) with different hold-out data (ZTF, not Gaia).
  **Consequences for us:** (i) cite it; (ii) do not claim "the first pre-registered test of
  periodic-quasar candidates"; our claims are the first test against Gaia epoch photometry and
  the first pre-registered test of the CRTS and PTF samples; (iii) it makes our "listed, not
  scored" treatment of Chen+2024 less of a gap.

The "overwhelmingly false positives" sentence is in the introduction of Huijse+ v2
(arXiv:2505.16884v2), citing El-Badry+2026; it is not a Gaia Collaboration paper. Huijse v2
says the method is "designed to scale directly" to DR4: an implied blind DR4 search by that
group, which is a different question from ours.
